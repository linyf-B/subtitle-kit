## Tagline（≤60 字符）

`Browser-only SRT tools—shift timing, no upload`

## 一句话描述

Free subtitle utilities for editors: shift SRT timing, export text, check line length, estimate read time—all in your browser, files never leave your device.

## 首评（Founder comment，~200 词）

I built Subtitle Kit because I kept fixing the same problem: subtitles exported fine but were uniformly 300–600 ms late after dubbing, and I didn't want to upload client SRT files to random converter sites.

Everything runs locally in JavaScript on Cloudflare Pages—paste or open a `.srt`, shift by ms, download. Same for SRT→text for translation handoff, caption line length for vertical video, and a simple read-time estimator.

No account, no paywall for the core tools. I'd love feedback from editors and translators on what to add next (VTT shift is on the list).

## FAQ 回复模板

**Q: Is it really offline/private?**  
A: Processing is in your browser tab. We don't receive your subtitle file content for conversion.

**Q: vs desktop NLE?**  
A: Not a replacement—quick fixes when you already have an SRT export.

**Q: Roadmap?**  
A: More guides, optional locales, VTT tools based on demand.
