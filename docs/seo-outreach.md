## 三版英文提交描述（复制到目录表单）

**50 words**  
Subtitle Kit offers free browser-based SRT tools: shift timing, export plain text, check caption line length, and estimate read time. Files stay on your device—nothing is uploaded for processing.

**150 words**  
Subtitle Kit is a free toolkit for video editors and translators working with SubRip (.srt) files. Shift all cues by milliseconds to fix sync after dubbing or re-export, convert subtitles to plain text for translation, check line length for vertical video, and estimate voice-over duration from a script. Every tool runs locally in your browser using JavaScript; subtitle content is not sent to our servers. No account required. Built for quick fixes without desktop software.

**300 words**  
Subtitle Kit helps creators and localization freelancers fix common subtitle problems in the browser. Use the SRT time shift tool when every line is consistently early or late—adjust by seconds or milliseconds and download a corrected file. Convert SRT to plain text when you need dialogue without timing codes for translators or script review. The caption line length checker flags lines that are too long for 9:16 short-form video. The read-time estimator plans narration length using adjustable words-per-minute presets. All processing happens on your device; we do not upload your subtitle files for server-side conversion. The site is free, fast, and designed for privacy-conscious workflows on Cloudflare Pages. Ideal for dubbing sync tweaks, translation prep, and social caption readability—not a replacement for full NLE suites, but a quick online helper when you need results in minutes.
